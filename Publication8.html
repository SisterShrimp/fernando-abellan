Research publication

Enhanced twisted arrow categories

Fernando Abellán and Walker Stern

Theory and Applications of Categories, Volume 39, 2023

Read paper ↗

Abstract

We give a construction for a (restricted version) of the cartesian fibration associated to the mapping category functor of an ∞-bicategory and use this construction to compute natural transformations between functors with source an ∞-category and target an ∞-bicategory.
