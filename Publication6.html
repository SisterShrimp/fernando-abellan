Research publication

2-Cartesian fibrations II: The Grothendieck construction for ∞-bicategories

Fernando Abellán and Walker Stern

Journal of the Institute of Mathematics of Jussieu

Read paper ↗

Abstract

In this work, we construct an ∞-bicategorical version of Lurie’s straightening-unstraightening adjunction. We provide a relative nerve construction in the case where the base is a strict 2-category and use this to prove a comparison to existing 2-categorical Grothendieck constructions.
